# Hoolio name screen — 2026-07-23

The owner selected `Hoolio` as the canonical character name on 2026-07-23.
This note records the screening done for that name.

## What was searched

- Web search for `"Hoolio" owl mascot OR character OR brand` found no
  prominent owl character, software mascot, or company brand using the name.
  The well-known owl-mascot field is occupied by Owly (Hootsuite), Duo
  (Duolingo), and the Hooters logo mark — none named or confusably close to
  `Hoolio`.
- `Hoolio` is an informal phonetic spelling of the Spanish given name
  `Julio`, which exists as personal names and occasional pet names. No
  software, AI, or developer-tools use was found.

## Rationale

The name is an owl pun (`Hoo-lio`) on the personal name Julio, consistent
with the character's dry, understated humor: the pun lives in the spelling,
and the character never acknowledges it out loud (see `CHARACTER.md`,
Voice — he never says "hoo").

## Sensitivity guardrails

The pun belongs only to the spelling and pronunciation. Do not give Hoolio a
mock accent, Spanish catchphrases, cultural costume, or ethnicity jokes. He is
an owl workshop character, not a representation of Hispanic or Latino people.

## Status

Use `Hoolio, the Little AI Company owl` as the approved character identity
for company use.

This note records a creative decision and a basic conflict search, not a
trademark registration or legal clearance. Complete a professional clearance
search before filing or expanding into merchandise.
